Elenoyze · Reference Hub

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Elenoyze Reference Hub (“the App”) is a personal-use project intended to help an authorized Pinterest account holder access their own boards and Pins for creative reference organization and motion design research. The Pinterest integration is currently being developed and is not yet operational.

Who operates the App

The App is operated as the Elenoyze Reference Hub project. For privacy questions or requests, contact delarov@yandex.ru.

Information the App may access

Once the integration is available and the user has authorized it, the App may request Pinterest board and Pin information available under the permissions the user grants, such as board names, Pin descriptions, source links, and image references. It will not request access to unrelated Pinterest accounts without authorization.

How information may be used

Authorized information will be used to display and organize creative references and, when requested by the user, assist with visual research and creative planning. If a user chooses to analyze references using a third-party AI service, relevant information may be submitted to that service for that request; the provider's privacy terms may then apply. This feature will not be enabled without an appropriate implementation and review of applicable Pinterest policies.

Storage and security

The public GitHub repository for this project is intended to contain source code, project documentation, and this policy—not Pinterest access tokens or a public archive of Pinterest content. Storage and retention details will be documented and this policy updated before any live Pinterest data processing begins. Any credentials used by the integration must be kept outside public source files.

Sharing

The App is not intended to sell personal information or share it for advertising. Data may be shared with services needed to carry out user-requested functionality, subject to the user's authorization, the providers' terms, and applicable Pinterest rules.

Your choices and requests

Users may revoke app access through their Pinterest account settings when an authorization is in place. For questions about access, correction, or deletion of information handled by this project, contact delarov@yandex.ru.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the App is developed. The updated version and date will be posted here before new data-handling features go live.
